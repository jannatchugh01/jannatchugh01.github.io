Air purifier market-entry analysis

Where should an air purifier startup launch first in India?

For a fictional startup, I combined air quality, disease surveillance, vehicle and EV registration, and population data. SQL joins, CTEs and date-based queries compared cities, pollutants, weekdays against weekends, and market size.

Finding: EV adoption alone does not explain better air quality. The strongest markets are cities where persistent poor AQI overlaps with a large population and a visible health burden, so I recommended targeting those first and designing filters around PM2.5 and PM10.

AI job market analysis

Do LLM-focused roles really pay more, and which skills do employers ask for?

I validated the dataset (missing values, duplicates, distributions), then compared salaries by experience, country, company size, work mode and role type. LLM and non-LLM roles were compared within each experience level, so the result isn't just an average distorted by seniority.

Finding: LLM roles show a consistent salary premium, remote roles pay about the same as on-site ones, and Python, machine learning and cloud are the most requested skills. These are patterns within this dataset, not universal salary benchmarks.
